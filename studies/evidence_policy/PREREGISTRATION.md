## 4. Baseline

`studies/capability/BASELINE.md`, generated by `studies/capability/tools/profile.py`: the current engine at
`TENDENCYSTEPS -1` and `0`, the SDK's strict filter, the engine with each structure's own setting, and three trivial
baselines (stated only, majority value, one-hop inverse), on every available structure, 5 seeds. Records the binary
sha256 and the tool commit.

## 5. Design

- **Development:** `company`, `hidden`, `gates`, `lineage`, `harvest`, `remainders`, `exceptions` (seeds 0 to 4).
- **Confirmation:** sealed worlds from a second, separately written generator covering every structure in the
  catalogue; seeds unseen until then.
- **Adversarial and metamorphic sets** for every world (C9: renaming, order, duplication, unrelated facts).
- **Controls:** `random_links`; `exceptions` with random exceptions.
- **Real data:** stress dpkg and Unicode; `strengths` (both codebases).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

The decision rules of the framework. Seed-spread thresholds are fixed from the baseline before confirmation. A change
is judged on its worst structure as well as its mean.

## 7. Compatibility

K-H0 measured and reported. Required to ship as opt-in; never counted as evidence of progress.

## 8. Replication

`strengths` (both codebases), Unicode and dpkg, with complete truth and no tuning.

## 9. Reporting

`RESULTS.md`, numbers recomputed by script: a capability x structure profile, risk-coverage curves per policy,
compatibility differences in their own section, diagnostics labelled, failures next to wins, cost.

## 10. Decision

By the decision-rules table. Changing the default policy is a separate, later decision needing two independent
replications.

## 11. Owner decisions

- 2026-11-23, nesting: yes. Strict answers must be a subset of tendency answers; strict admits validated or strong
  tendencies by their evidence.
- 2026-11-23, replacement: `POLICY strict` replaces `TENDENCYSTEPS 0` and SDK `strict=True`; old controls remain as
  aliases until a major version (compatibility track).
- 2026-11-23, validation: an evaluation result with its method recorded; Round 1 uses internal split-half replication
  (hash or blocked), never described as independent-source validation. Built-in semantics: legacy exact; tendency by
  evidence thresholds without replication; strict with minimum evidence, acceptable agreement and replication.
- 2026-11-23, custom policies: first-class in SDK and CLI; built-ins are reference profiles (`docs/POLICIES.md`).
