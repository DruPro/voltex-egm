Generated by `studies/capability/tools/profile.py`; do not edit by hand. Engine binary sha256 `ab6995a985c2390d…`, tool commit `776e3b3`, 5 seeds per world, 2026-11-23.

Scored through the evaluation interface (`tools/interface.py`) against each world's complete truth. Values are mean ± sd over seeds. "undef." = nothing to divide by (no new facts, nothing missing, no undeterminable queries labelled, no corruptions). AURC = mean risk (1 − precision) over the engine's own ranking of its new answers: lower is better, read it with `new` beside it. P@R50 = precision when the ranking first reaches 50% recall. `egm-matched` is tuned per structure and is labelled so. Trivial baselines are given the relation phrases, which EGM must discover.
